## Removed case studies; replaced Lagos with Nigeria (commit `fa1da45`)

Author's request: "Remove the case studies section entirely, since I do not have any
actual completed works to display" + replace every "Lagos" with "Nigeria", keeping
references consistent.

### Asked before acting — three decisions were genuinely ambiguous

Removing case studies had a cascade the request did not anticipate, so I mapped the
blast radius first and asked three questions. Answers:

1. **Testimonials → remove too.** They were attributed to the same four clients as
   the deleted case studies (Nairaflow/Kobo/Ajo/Owo), so keeping them would have
   contradicted the removal. (Note: these were the quotes I had just re-derived from
   `projects.ts` in `9bf621a` — that deduplication work was undone by deleting both
   sides, which is the correct outcome.)
2. **Stats → keep all three as-is**, including "+28% median conversion lift across
   projects" which now has no displayed work behind it. Author's call; left untouched.
3. **Hero CTA → point to Services**, now "What I do".
